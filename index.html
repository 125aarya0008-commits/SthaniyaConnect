HOW IT WORKS

Reporting a problem takes less time than typing it into your group chat.

Here's exactly what happens, step by step — simple enough to explain to anyone.

1

Open the map

Click "Report an issue near you." You'll see a dark map with colored dots — every dot is a problem someone already reported nearby.

2

Pick what's wrong

Tap one button: Power cut, Water leak, Streetlight, or Other. That's it — no long form, no signing up, no typing an essay.

3

Say where

Type the name of your area (like "MIDC Road, Karad") and either tap "Use my location" or click the exact spot on the map.

Tip: the more people who name the same area the same way, the faster the pattern shows up.
4

Add a quick note (optional)

If you've noticed something useful — like "this happens every Tuesday evening" — add one line. It helps everyone see the pattern faster.

5

Submit — and watch it appear

Your pin drops on the map immediately. If enough reports build up in your area, its reliability grade updates right there, live, for anyone to see.

6

Share the score, not just the complaint

Instead of forwarding a screenshot of a WhatsApp message, share the live scoreboard link. A public "D grade" gets noticed in a way a private complaint never does.
